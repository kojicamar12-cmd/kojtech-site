FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Kojtech AI do?

We build and run AI for businesses: AI voice receptionists, custom AI agents, chatbots, lead generation, CRM and booking automation. We also handle websites, apps and day-to-day IT, so you have one partner for all your tech.

Can Kojtech AI be our IT department?

Yes. With a monthly partnership we look after your website, email, software, security and AI tools, give you one point of contact and send a monthly report.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free 30-minute audit, we give you a fixed price in writing. Projects are quoted one time, and ongoing support is a flat monthly fee.

Will an AI receptionist replace my staff?

Usually not. It handles after-hours calls, repetitive questions and booking, and transfers anything complex to your team, so your people spend time on the work that matters.

Is this only for big companies?

No. Most of our clients are small and local. Start with one system, like an AI receptionist or a new website, and add more when you're ready.

Can one AI handle calls, texts and website chat?

Yes. One AI agent can work across phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and your website, using one shared knowledge base and CRM.

How fast can we launch?

Many AI receptionists and websites go live within days to a few weeks. Larger custom builds get a clear timeline in your proposal.

What if I don't like the work?

We'll fix it. If you're still not happy, you get your money back.
